Career Profile

My name is Carlos Henrique Motta, and I'm a Senior Software Developer/UX from Brazil (🇧🇷). I've been working with web development since 2017.

I began my career with front-end internships at TOTVS, B2ML Sistemas and Amagafa TECH, before joining Solidaridad Network as my first full-time front-end role — using technology to create a positive impact in people's lives through digital inclusion, building mobile/hybrid apps that supported farmers and field extension officers on a global scale.

At Emplifi (formerly Socialbakers), I developed and maintained UI components, managed a custom CSS UI library published as an npm package, and contributed to the Soul Design System — an initiative that unified the company's visual standards across its products.

At Reply, I worked on the Axulus platform and airport solutions for clients such as Fraport and Munich Airport, with Angular 15+, Tailwind CSS and PrimeNG — including an AI-based solution for detecting ULDs and trains.

Currently, at Zup Innovation, I work as a Senior Angular Developer on a large digital transformation project for Itaú, building robust interfaces with Angular, AI-augmented spec-driven development workflows and Playwright end-to-end tests.

My goal is to use technology as a tool for empowerment, helping people see it as a transformative force in their lives.
